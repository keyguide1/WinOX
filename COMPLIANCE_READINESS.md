# Compliance Readiness

This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice or a claim of licensing.
All unresolved items are **REQUIRES LEGAL/REGULATORY VERIFICATION** before any
real-money activation.

- [ ] Applicable gaming/competition classification reviewed.
- [ ] Payment-provider onboarding and terms reviewed.
- [ ] KYC and age-verification requirements reviewed.
- [ ] Jurisdiction and restricted-country rules reviewed.
- [ ] AML/suspicious-activity requirements reviewed where applicable.
- [ ] Tax and prize-reporting treatment reviewed.
- [ ] Privacy, retention, access, and deletion requirements reviewed.
- [ ] Consumer terms, fees, refunds, disputes, and disclosures reviewed.
- [ ] Responsible-competition controls reviewed.
- [ ] Security assessment and incident obligations reviewed.

Until every applicable item is verified by the appropriate professionals,
`REAL_MONEY_ENABLED=false` must remain enforced.
